DROP YOUR ASSETS HERE
=====================

Place these two files in this folder:

  map.png        → Your map image (any size)
  character.png  → Your character spritesheet

Then open game.js and update the CONFIG.sprite section:
  - frameWidth  : width of ONE frame in pixels
  - frameHeight : height of ONE frame in pixels
  - scale       : how big to render the character (2 = 2x zoom)
  - animations  : map state names to spritesheet rows

The game will automatically detect and switch from placeholder to
your real assets on next page reload.

When you're ready, just tell me and I'll help you configure
the spritesheet settings!
